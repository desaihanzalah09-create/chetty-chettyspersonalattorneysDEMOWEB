Road Accident Fund (RAF) Claims

Aggressive Legal Action to Recover Full Financial Restitution

Claiming Max Compensation From the RAF

If you have been injured on South African roads as a driver, passenger, pedestrian, or cyclist, you have a statutory right to claim compensation from the Road Accident Fund for injuries sustained due to another driver's negligence.

  • Past and Future Medical Expenses
  • Loss of Past and Future Earnings / Income
  • General Damages for Pain, Suffering, and Loss of Amenities
  • Loss of Support Claims for Dependents of Deceased Victims

Injured in a Motor Vehicle Crash?

Strict time limits apply. Identified vehicle claims prescribe in 3 years; hit-and-run claims prescribe in 2 years.
