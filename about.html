About Chetty & Chetty Incorporated

Committed to Integrity, Legal Excellence, and Relentless Advocacy

OUR FIRM

Defending the Rights of Injured South Africans

Based in Pietermaritzburg, Chetty & Chetty Incorporated is a premier boutique law firm devoted exclusively to personal injury litigation, statutory claims, and medical liability law.

When an unexpected catastrophe or injury occurs, victims and their families are often left navigating immense physical pain, psychological trauma, and severe financial distress. Our mission is to restore stability and financial security by taking on state institutions, insurance funds, and corporate entities with relentless legal determination.
