Road Accident Fund (RAF) Claims

Restoring Your Future Following Motor Vehicle Collision Injuries

Expert Representation Against the RAF

If you or a loved one suffered serious injuries in a vehicle crash on South African roads, you are legally entitled to claim compensation for medical expenses, loss of earnings, and general damages for pain and suffering.

  • Driver, Passenger, Pedestrian, and Cyclist Claims
  • Loss of Income & Loss of Support Claims for Dependents
  • Past & Future Medical Expenses Recoupment
  • Expert Actuarial and Medical Assessments

Injured in a Car Accident?

Strict statutory time frames apply to RAF claims (3 years for identified vehicles, 2 years for hit-and-run accidents).

Medical Negligence & Malpractice

Holding Doctors, Surgeons, and Hospitals Accountable for Breach of Duty

Medical Malpractice Claims Specialist

Medical negligence occurs when healthcare providers fail to adhere to established standards of care, resulting in severe physical injury, disability, or wrongful death.

Birth Injuries

Cerebral palsy, hypoxia, and birth trauma caused by delayed emergency interventions or delivery negligence.

Surgical Errors

Organ perforation, retained surgical instruments, or incorrect surgical procedures.

Misdiagnosis

Failure to diagnose critical illnesses or catastrophic medication/anesthesia mistakes.

Personal Injury & Slip and Fall Claims

Premises Liability & Public Safety Negligence

Slips, Trips, Falls & Public Injuries

Property owners, shopping centers, municipal bodies, and commercial businesses owe a duty of care to ensure public spaces are safe. If you suffer injury due to broken stairs, wet unmonitored floors, uncovered manholes, or dog attacks, Chetty & Chetty Inc. will pursue your damages aggressively.

Unlawful Arrest & Police Assault

Protection of Constitutional Rights & State Liability Claims

Action Against SAPS & Metro Police

If you have been illegally detained, falsely arrested, or subjected to excessive force or assault by police or law enforcement officers, we file constitutional and tort damages against the Minister of Police and local municipalities.

Product Liability & Defective Goods

Consumer Protection Act Rights & Compensation

Defective Products & Hazardous Items

Under the Consumer Protection Act (CPA), manufacturers, distributors, and retailers are strictly liable for injuries caused by unsafe, defective, or failing equipment, machinery, and commercial products.

Wrongful Death Claims

Supporting Families After Tragic Fatalities

Loss of Support for Spouses & Children

When a primary breadwinner dies due to someone else's negligence or reckless behavior, surviving dependents have a legal right to compensation for loss of maintenance, support, and funeral costs.

Workplace Injuries & COIDA Claims

Occupational Injuries, Machinery Accidents & Industrial Hazards

Compensation for Occupational Injuries

We assist workers in navigating claims through the Compensation Commissioner under COIDA, as well as pursuing third-party civil liability suits where gross third-party negligence contributed to workplace injury.
